# Word-by-word study data

Generated by `tools/build_words.py` from the STEPBible data
(https://STEPBible.github.io/STEPBible-Data), created by STEPBible.org based on work at
Tyndale House, Cambridge, and licensed CC BY 4.0.

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| TAHOT — Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT | Old Testament words (Leningrad Codex, following the Qere as translators do) |
| TAGNT — Translators Amalgamated Greek NT | New Testament words, in the Textus Receptus wording used by the reader |
| TBESH / TBESG — brief Hebrew and Greek lexicons | `data/lexicon/` dictionary entries |
| TEHMC / TEGMC — morphology code expansions | `grammar-heb.json`, `grammar-gk.json` |

## Files

- `<book>/<chapter>.json` — `{"<verse>": [word, …]}`. Verses use the original-language numbering:
  Hebrew numbering in the Old Testament (Psalm titles are verse 1, Malachi has 3 chapters),
  and the reader's Textus Receptus numbering in the New Testament.
  Each word is `[text, transliteration, english, strongs, grammar, note?]`. In Hebrew words, `/`
  separates prefixes, root and suffixes in every field, and the root's Strong's number is in `{braces}`.
- `index.json` — per book: language, chapter count, and `map` from English (KJV) `ch:v` to the
  original-language `ch:v` where they differ.
- `../lexicon/<G|H><n>.json` — dictionary entries for Strong's numbers `n*100` to `n*100+99`:
  `[lemma, transliteration, gloss, definition-html]`.

## Changes made to the STEPBible data

- Greek: words absent from the Textus Receptus are left out, unless the reader's own TR text has them.
  Where the TR spells, orders or words a passage differently, the TR form and order are used and the
  other manuscripts' reading is kept as a note on the word. Words are assigned to the reader's verse
  divisions where editions divide verses differently.
- Hebrew: words added from the Septuagint (STEPBible type X) and stray manuscript variants (type V)
  are left out. Paragraph and punctuation marks are removed from word text.
- Lexicon definitions keep only bold, italic, line breaks and Bible references.

Please send corrections to the underlying data to STEPBible@Gmail.com.
