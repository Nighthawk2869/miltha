THE MISSION
THE WORD IS FREE.
It always has been. It always will be. MILTHA exists to make sure it stays that way.
"Ho, every one that thirsteth, come ye to the waters, and he that hath no money; come ye, buy, and eat; yea, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price."

WHO WE ARE
MILTHA is currently a one-person mission. One builder, one vision, one conviction: that every believer on earth — regardless of language, background, or resources — deserves access to the Word of Christ in its earliest, most authentic form.
Every line of code, every piece of scholarship, every page of this platform was built by hand — with no corporate backing, no investor funding, and no team. Just a calling and the tools to answer it.
The goal is to keep MILTHA free forever. No paywalls. No subscriptions. No algorithm deciding what you see. The Word, open to all, always.
"Freely ye have received, freely give."
MATTHEW 10:8 · KJV 1611
