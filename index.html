THE MISSION

A digital sanctuary built on the earliest manuscripts of Scripture.

Jesus Christ spoke Aramaic. His apostles carried the gospel in Greek and Aramaic across the ancient world. The earliest complete New Testament — the Peshitta — was written in the language Christ Himself used.

MILTHA — ܡܸܠܺܬ݂ܵܐ — is the Aramaic word for "The Word" from John 1:1 in the Peshitta. It means far more than "word" — it carries the weight of manifestation, substance, the very essence of God made known.

We are building a platform that presents the scriptures and teachings of Jesus Christ as accurate, authentic, and accessible to all people — rooted in the earliest manuscript evidence, at no cost, for all time. Christ is King, and His Word endures.
