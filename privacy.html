PRIVACY
What MILTHA keeps, who can see it, and how to remove it.

MILTHA is free and has no advertising. We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing, and we do not use tracking or analytics tools.

READING WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

You can read the Bible, study word by word, use the Daily Word, the Library and the Knowledge Engine without signing up. MILTHA itself does not record who you are or what you read.

Like every website, the computers that deliver the pages see basic technical details, such as your internet (IP) address and browser. The site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps short-term server logs for security and to limit abuse. The site's lettering comes from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for the font files.

IF YOU CREATE AN ACCOUNT

When you join, we keep:

  • Your name and email address — to sign you in and to send the emails you need (confirming your address, resetting your password).
  • Your password — stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by Netlify Identity; no one at MILTHA can see it.
  • What you save — the verses you save, your notes on them, and the days you mark the Daily Word as read.

Accounts are run by Netlify Identity, and your saved verses, notes and reading are kept in Netlify's storage. Netlify is based in the United States.

Your sign-in is remembered in your own browser (its local storage) so you stay signed in. MILTHA does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

WHO CAN SEE WHAT

  • Your notes and reading are private. They are never shown to other members or the public.
  • Community posts and replies show your name, the passage and what you wrote, and once approved they are public for anyone to read.
  • Your email address is never shown publicly. Moderators see it beside posts waiting for approval, so they can contact you if needed.
  • Moderation alerts: when you post or reply, your name, the passage and your text are emailed to the moderators so they can review it.
  • The people who run MILTHA can technically reach the stored data. We look at it only when needed to keep the site working, to deal with abuse, or if the law requires it.

THE KNOWLEDGE ENGINE

When you ask the Knowledge Engine a question, your question (and the earlier questions and answers on the page, for context) is sent to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, through Netlify's AI Gateway. MILTHA does not save your questions or link them to your account. Please don't type personal details into your questions.

DONATIONS

Gifts are made on Stripe's secure payment pages. Stripe handles your payment details; MILTHA never sees your card number.

REMOVING YOUR INFORMATION

  • On My Account you can delete all your saved verses, notes and reading at any time, and change your name or password.
  • To close your account completely, or to have a post removed, email talk@miltha.org and we will do it.
  • You can also ask us what information we hold about you.

CHILDREN

Anyone may read MILTHA. Accounts are meant for people aged 13 and over; younger readers are welcome to use the site without one.

CHANGES AND QUESTIONS

If this page changes, the date below will change too. Questions are welcome at talk@miltha.org.
